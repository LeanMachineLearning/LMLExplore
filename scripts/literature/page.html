Literature

The results of the literature that LMLExplore's declarations and its roadmaps cite, at commit {{commit12}} of LMLExplore and {{roadmaps-commit12}} of LMLRoadmaps. {{summary}}.

A declaration is linked to a result when it cites it, as [GK16, Theorem 14], in its docstring or in the list of main statements of its module. A link says that the declaration and the result correspond, not how: the declaration may state the result exactly, more generally, or one part of it, or be a step of its proof. A result that the roadmaps cite and no declaration does is not linked yet: it may be formalized without the citation, or not at all. The links are also published as annotations.jsonl ({{annotations}} annotations), in the Literature Links format.

The location of a numbered result

The subject of each annotation is a numbered result of a work. Its location has "profile": "{{profile}}", a label and optionally a part. label is the result as the work prints it, such as Theorem 14, Lemma A.1, Section 4.3, or (8.2) for an equation, in the numbering of the revision that the end's version names, or of the published work when it names none. part narrows it, such as (a). An annotation whose kind is proof is about the proof of the result.

The extension {{extension}} of an annotation names the LMLRoadmaps items that cite its result, as Identification 5.2, the second target of the layer 5 of the Identification roadmap, with the commit of LMLRoadmaps they were read at. An annotation whose object is empty records a result that the roadmaps cite and no declaration does.
