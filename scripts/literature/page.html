Literature

The results of the literature that LMLExplore's declarations cite, and the declarations that cite them, at commit {{commit12}}: {{summary}}.

Each link comes from a citation such as [GK16, Theorem 14] in a declaration's docstring, or in the list of main statements of its module. It says that the declaration and the result correspond, not how: the declaration may state the result exactly, more generally, or one part of it, or be a step of its proof. The links are also published as annotations.jsonl, in the Literature Links format.

The location of a numbered result

The subject of each annotation is a numbered result of a work. Its location has "profile": "{{profile}}", a label and optionally a part. label is the result as the work prints it, such as Theorem 14, Lemma A.1, Section 4.3, or (8.2) for an equation, in the numbering of the revision that the end's version names, or of the published work when it names none. part narrows it, such as (a). An annotation whose kind is proof is about the proof of the result.
